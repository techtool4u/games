How to play
  • On your turn, roll as often as you like. Every roll adds to this turn's total.
  • Roll a 1 and you lose the whole turn total, and your turn ends.
  • Press Hold to bank the turn total into your score and pass the dice.
  • Two dice: a single 1 loses the turn; double 1s (snake eyes) also wipes your whole score; any other double means you must roll again.
  • First to reach the target wins.
  • Keyboard: R or Space rolls, H or Enter holds.
