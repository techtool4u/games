Paddle Ball

How to play
  • Keep the ball in play with your paddle and slip it past your rival's. Miss and they score.
  • Where the ball hits your paddle sets its angle, and every rally makes it a little faster.
  • Your paddle is at the bottom: drag along the bottom half of the court, move the mouse, or use ← and →.
  • Two players: the top player drags in the top half (or uses A and D), the bottom player in the bottom half (or ← and →).
  • P pauses.
