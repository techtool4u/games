Hangman

How to play
  • Guess the hidden word one letter at a time. Tap a letter or type it on your keyboard.
  • Every wrong letter adds a piece to the drawing. Finish the word before the drawing is complete.
  • Easy: shorter words and 8 lives. Medium: 6 lives. Hard: 6 lives and the category is hidden.
  • Stuck? Reveal a letter, but it costs one life.
  • 2 players: one player types a secret word (and an optional clue), then passes the device to the guesser. Take turns.
  • Wins, losses and your streak are saved on this device.
