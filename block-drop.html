How to play
  • Shapes fall into the well. Move and rotate them so they fill complete rows; full rows disappear.
  • Clearing more rows at once scores more: 100, 300, 500 or 800 points, times the level. Every 10 rows the level goes up and pieces fall faster.
  • Hold swaps the falling piece with the one in the hold box (once per piece). The faint outline shows where the piece will land.
  • The game ends when a new piece has no room to appear.
  • Keyboard: ← → move, ↑ or X rotates, Z rotates the other way, ↓ soft drop, Space hard drop, C holds, P pauses.
  • Touch: the buttons, or swipe on the well (left/right to move, down to drop, tap to rotate).
