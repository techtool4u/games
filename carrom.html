Carrom

Drag right & let go
How to play
  • One player has the 9 white coins, the other the 9 black. White strikes first. Flick the striker to knock your coins into the four corner pockets.
  • Move the striker along your baseline by dragging it, or with the Striker slider. Aim by dragging on the board: the line shows where it will go. ◀ ▶ fine-tune the aim. Then drag the power bar right and let go to flick.
  • The striker must go forward from your baseline. Coins behind your line can still be reached off the side or far edges.
  • Pocket one of your coins and you go again.
  • The queen (red) can be pocketed once you have pocketed a coin of your own. She must be covered: pocket one of your coins in the same shot or the very next one, or she goes back to the center.
  • Foul: if the striker goes into a pocket, your turn ends and one of your pocketed coins comes back to the center.
  • Win by pocketing all your coins once the queen has been covered (by either player). You score a point for each of your rival's coins left, plus 3 if you hold the queen. Your last coin can't go in before the queen is covered: if it does, it comes back.
  • Online: one player hosts, the other joins with the game code. The 2D and 3D boards can play each other.
  • Keyboard: arrows aim (Shift for bigger steps), A and D move the striker, hold Space to build power and let go to flick.
