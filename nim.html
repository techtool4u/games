Nim

How to play
  • On your turn, take one or more sticks, all from a single row.
  • Tap a stick to pick it and every stick to its right in that row, then press Take.
  • In the usual rule, whoever is forced to take the last stick loses. Turn on "last stick wins" for normal play.
  • Nim has a perfect strategy based on adding the row sizes in binary without carrying. The Perfect computer knows it, so you need to start from a winning position and never slip.
  • Keyboard: ↑ ↓ choose a row, ← → change how many, Enter takes.
