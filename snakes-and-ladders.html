Snakes and Ladders

Roll and race to square 100. Ladders carry you up, snakes slide you down. Pure luck, all the way to the top.

    How to play
    • Everyone starts off the board. Take turns rolling the die and moving your pawn that many squares, following the numbers from 1 up to 100.
    • Land at the foot of a ladder and climb to its top. Land on a snake's head and slide down to its tail.
    • Rolling a 6 gives you another roll.
    • With Exact roll on, you must land exactly on 100: if the roll is too big you bounce back by the extra squares. With it off, any roll that reaches 100 wins.
    • Keyboard: Space or R rolls.
