Nine Men's Morris

Place your nine pieces, then slide them along the lines. Line up three in a row to form a mill and take one of your rival's pieces.

How to play
  • Placing: take turns putting your nine pieces on any empty point. White starts.
  • Moving: once every piece is placed, slide one of yours along a line to a neighboring empty point.
  • Mills: three of your pieces in a straight line form a mill. Each new mill lets you remove one rival piece, but not one that is in a mill unless every rival piece is.
  • Flying: with only three pieces left you may jump to any empty point.
  • You lose with fewer than three pieces, or when you cannot move. A position repeated three times, or 50 moves each without a capture, is a draw.
  • Keyboard: arrows move between points, Enter or Space selects, U undoes.
