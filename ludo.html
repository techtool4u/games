Ludo

Roll a six to bring a pawn out, race it around the board and up your home column. Land on a rival to send it back to its yard.

How to play
  • Each player has four pawns waiting in their yard. Roll a 6 to move a pawn out onto your colored start square.
  • Pawns travel clockwise once around the board, then turn up their own colored home column to the center. You need the exact roll to finish.
  • Land on a rival's pawn to send it back to its yard. Pawns on a start square or a star square are safe and can share it.
  • Rolling a 6, capturing a pawn, or getting a pawn home earns another roll. Three 6s in a row lose the turn.
  • The first player with all four pawns home wins.
  • Keyboard: Space or R rolls, arrows choose a pawn, Enter moves it.
