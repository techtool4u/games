Hearts

Scores

How to play
  • Avoid points. Each heart you take is 1 point and the queen of spades is 13. Lowest score when someone reaches the target wins.
  • Before each hand, pass three cards: to the left, then right, then across, then no pass, and repeat.
  • Whoever holds the 2♣ leads it. You must follow the suit led if you can; otherwise play anything. The highest card of the led suit wins the trick and leads next.
  • No points can be played on the first trick (unless you have nothing else), and hearts can't be led until one has been played.
  • Shoot the moon: take all 13 hearts and the queen of spades, and everyone else gets 26 instead of you.
  • Keyboard: ← → choose a card, Enter plays it (or picks it for passing).
