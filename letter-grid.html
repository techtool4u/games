Letter Grid

Your words

How to play
  • Find words by chaining letters that touch, across, up, down or diagonally. Each square can be used once per word.
  • Drag across the letters and let go, or tap them one at a time and tap the last one again (or press Enter). You can also just type a word.
  • Words must be at least 3 letters (4 on the 5×5 grid). Points: 3 and 4 letters 1, 5 letters 2, 6 letters 3, 7 letters 5, 8 or more 11.
  • With 2 to 4 players, everyone plays the same grid in turn. Words that more than one player found don't score.
  • Any word in the dictionary counts. When time runs out you'll see the everyday words that were hiding in the grid.
