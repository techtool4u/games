How to play
  • Collect books: all four cards of one rank. Each player starts with 7 cards (5 with four or more players).
  • On your turn, tap a rank in your hand, then tap a player to ask them for it. You can only ask for ranks you already hold.
  • If they have any, they must hand them all over and you ask again. If not, they say "Go fish!" and you draw from the pond. If you fish the rank you asked for, show it and go again.
  • Completed books are laid down right away. If your hand runs out, you draw a card from the pond (if any are left).
  • When all 13 books are made, the player with the most books wins.
  • The computers remember what's been asked: forgetful ones lose track quickly, card counters remember everything.
  • Keyboard: ← → choose a rank, 1–4 ask that player, Enter asks the only rival.
