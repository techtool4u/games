Horse Race

How to play
  • Eleven horses race, numbered 2 to 12. Each roll of two dice moves the horse with that total one step. 7 comes up most often, so even with the longest track it is the favorite; 2 and 12 come up least, so they are long shots despite their short tracks.
  • Before each race, every player places 3 chips on any horses (tap a lane to bet; the same horse more than once is fine). The number on the right of each lane is what a chip pays if that horse wins. The payouts are fair, so a long shot pays a lot and a favorite pays a little.
  • Then the race is run: players take turns rolling (or turn on Auto-roll). The first horse past the post wins, and every chip on it pays out.
  • After the last race, the player with the most winnings wins. Ties share the win.
  • Keyboard: Space or R rolls.
