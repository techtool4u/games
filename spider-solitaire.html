Spider Solitaire

Score 500Moves 0Time 0:00Sets 0/8
How to play
  • Build each column downward: you can put any card on one that is exactly one rank higher (a 7 on an 8).
  • A run of cards in the same suit, in order, moves together. Mixed-suit runs must be moved one card at a time.
  • Complete a run from King down to Ace in one suit and it leaves the table. Clear all 8 runs to win.
  • Tap the stock (top right) to deal one new card onto every column. Every column needs at least one card first.
  • Tap a card to send it to the best spot automatically, or drag it where you want. Undo is unlimited.
  • Score: 500 to start, −1 per move or deal, +100 per completed run. Your game is saved if you leave.
