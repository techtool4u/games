Reversi

Place a disc so it traps a line of your rival's discs, and they all flip to your color. Most discs at the end wins.

How to play
  • Black moves first. Place a disc next to an opposing disc so that one or more straight lines of opposing discs end in one of yours. Every trapped disc flips.
  • Lines can run across, up and down, or diagonally, and one move can flip several lines at once.
  • If you have no legal move you must pass. When neither player can move, the player with more discs wins.
  • Corners can never be flipped, so they are worth a lot. The squares next to an empty corner are risky because they can hand it to your rival.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, Enter or Space places, U undoes.
