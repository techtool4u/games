Star Checkers

Race your ten marbles across the star into the opposite point. Hop over any marble, and chain hops together for big leaps.

How to play
  • Each player starts with ten marbles in one point of the star. The goal is the point straight across.
  • On your turn, move one marble: either one step to an empty neighboring hole, or a hop over a neighboring marble (anyone's) into the empty hole just beyond it.
  • Hops can be chained, in any direction, as long as each one lands in an empty hole. Tap your marble to see every place it can reach, then tap one. Marbles are never captured.
  • The first player to fill their goal point wins. If a rival's marble is still sitting in your goal, filling every other hole of it also counts as a win.
  • Keyboard: arrows choose a marble or a destination, Enter picks, Escape cancels, U undoes.
