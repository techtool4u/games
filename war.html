How to play
  • The deck is split evenly. Each round, both players flip their top card. The higher card wins both, and they go to the bottom of the winner's pile. Aces are high.
  • If the cards tie, it's war: each player puts three cards face down and one face up. The higher face-up card takes the whole pile. Ties can lead to double wars.
  • If you don't have enough cards for a war, your last card is used as the face-up one.
  • Quick games end when the piles have been played through once: whoever won more cards wins. Classic games go on until one player has all 52, which can take a while, so try Auto-flip.
  • 2 players: each taps their own Flip button (or presses A and L).
  • Keyboard: Space flips.
