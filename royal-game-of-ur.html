Royal Game of Ur

How to play
  • Race all seven of your pieces along your path and off the board before your rival does. Your path runs along your own row (from the middle toward the left end), up the shared middle row, then back along your own row on the right to the exit.
  • Roll four pyramid dice: each one with its white tip up counts 1, so you move 0 to 4 squares. Tap a highlighted piece to move it (a piece in your waiting pile enters the board).
  • Land exactly on a rival piece in the middle row to knock it back to the start. You can't land on your own piece.
  • The flowered squares are rosettes: landing on one gives you another roll, and a piece on the middle rosette can't be knocked off.
  • You need the exact number to bear a piece off. If you can't move, your turn passes.
