Snake

Swipe, use the arrows below, or press an arrow key to start.

How to play
  • Steer the snake to eat the red apples. Each one makes it longer and scores a point.
  • Golden apples appear now and then for 5 points, but only for a few seconds.
  • Don't run into yourself. With solid walls, the edges are deadly too; with wrap around you come out the other side.
  • The snake speeds up a little as it grows.
  • Controls: swipe on the playfield, the direction pad, arrow keys or WASD. Space or P pauses.
