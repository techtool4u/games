Your turn
How to play
  • Roll six dice. Set aside at least one scoring die (tap them), then either roll the rest again or bank the points.
  • If a roll has nothing that scores, that's a farkle: you lose everything from this turn.
  • Score with all six dice and you get hot dice: roll all six again and keep building.
  • Each roll's scoring dice count only within that roll.
  • First to the target wins (everyone else gets one last turn to beat it).
  • Keyboard: 1–6 pick dice, R rolls, B banks.
