How to play
  • Fill every empty square so each row, each column and each box contains every symbol exactly once: 1 to 9 on the classic 9×9 grid, 1 to 9 and A to C on 12×12 (boxes of 3×4), and 1 to 9 and A to G on 16×16.
  • Tap a square, then a number. Turn on Notes to pencil in candidates; they clear themselves when you place that digit nearby.
  • Squares in the same row, column and box are shaded, and every copy of the selected digit is highlighted.
  • With Show mistakes on, wrong numbers turn red. Hints fill in one correct square.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution. Your progress is saved if you leave.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, 1–9 (and A–G on big grids) fill, 0 or Backspace erases, N toggles notes, U undoes, H hints.
