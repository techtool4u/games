How to play
  • Aim with your finger or mouse and let go (or click) to shoot. Bubbles bounce off the side walls.
  • Make a group of three or more of the same color to pop them. Any bubbles left hanging with nothing holding them up fall too, for bonus points.
  • Every few shots a new row pushes down from the top. If the bubbles reach the line at the bottom, the game is over.
  • Tap the launcher (or press Space) to swap the current and next bubble. Clear the board to go up a level.
  • Keyboard: ← → aim, ↑ or Enter shoots.
