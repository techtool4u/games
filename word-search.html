How to play
  • Every word in the list is hidden in the grid in a straight line.
  • Drag across a word to circle it, or tap its first letter and then its last letter.
  • Easy words run across and down. Medium adds diagonals. Hard adds words spelled backwards, and has more of them.
  • Hint flashes the first letter of a word you haven't found yet.
