How to play
  • Move your bat by dragging anywhere on the table: left and right, and up and down to step in or back. The bat swings by itself when the ball reaches it.
  • Aim by moving the bat sideways as you hit: swipe right to send the ball right. Hitting with the edge of the bat angles it too. Swipe forward as you hit for a faster, deeper shot, but too hard or too wide and it goes out or into the net.
  • Serve by tapping the table. The serve bounces once on your side, then on your opponent's. Move the bat sideways first to aim it.
  • After the serve, the ball must land on the other side, and each player lets it bounce once on their side before hitting it back. Miss, hit it out, into the net, or let it bounce twice, and the point goes to your opponent.
  • A game goes to 11, and you must win by 2. The serve changes every 2 points (every point from 10–10).
  • 2 players on one device: the table is shown from above; each player drags in their own half.
  • Online: one player hosts and the other joins with the game code. The host's device runs the game, so a good connection helps.
  • Keyboard: arrows move the bat, Space serves.
