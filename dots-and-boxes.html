Dots and Boxes

Take turns joining two dots. Close the fourth side of a box to claim it and go again. Most boxes wins.

Boxes

How to play
  • On your turn, draw one line between two neighboring dots, across or down.
  • If your line closes the fourth side of a box (or two boxes), you claim them and must draw another line.
  • When every line is drawn, whoever claimed the most boxes wins.
  • Tip: don't draw the third side of a box unless you have to. Late in the game, long chains of boxes decide everything; sometimes it's worth giving away two boxes to keep control.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move between lines, Enter or Space draws, U undoes.
