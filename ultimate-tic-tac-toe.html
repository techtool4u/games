How to play
  • The big board is made of nine small tic-tac-toe boards. Win a small board by getting three in a row on it.
  • Win the game by winning three small boards in a row on the big board.
  • The catch: the square you play in sends your opponent to the matching small board. Play top-right in a small board, and they must play in the top-right board next.
  • If you're sent to a board that's already won or full, you can play in any open board. Highlighted boards are where you may play.
  • A won board can't be played in any more.
