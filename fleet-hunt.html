How to play
  • Each side hides five ships: a carrier (5 squares), battleship (4), cruiser (3), submarine (3) and destroyer (2). Ships sit in straight lines and don't overlap.
  • To arrange yours, tap a ship to pick it, then tap a square to move it there. Tap the picked ship again (or press Rotate) to turn it. Shuffle places them at random.
  • Take turns firing at a square on the enemy chart. A red burst is a hit, a white dot is a miss. When every square of a ship is hit, it sinks and is revealed.
  • First to sink the whole enemy fleet wins.
  • Keyboard: arrows aim, Enter or Space fires.
