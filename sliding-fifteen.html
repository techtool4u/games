Sliding Fifteen

Moves 0Time 0:00
How to play
  • Put the tiles back in order, 1 at the top left, reading across, with the gap in the bottom-right corner.
  • Tap a tile next to the gap to slide it in. Tap a tile further along the same row or column to slide the whole line at once. You can also swipe.
  • Tiles already in their home square get a green rim.
  • Tip: solve the top row, then the left column, and repeat on the smaller puzzle that's left.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move the tile next to the gap in that direction.
