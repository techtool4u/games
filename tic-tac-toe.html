How to play
  • Take turns marking an empty square. X goes first.
  • Get three of your marks in a row, across, down or diagonally, to win.
  • If all nine squares fill up with no line, it's a draw. Played perfectly, every game is a draw, so see if you can catch the computer out on Easy or Medium.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, Enter or Space places, N starts a new round.
