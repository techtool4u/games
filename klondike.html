Klondike Solitaire

Score 0Moves 0Time 0:00
How to play
  • Build the four foundations (top right) up by suit, from ace to king. Move all 52 cards there to win.
  • On the seven columns, stack cards downward in alternating colors: a red 6 on a black 7. Any face-up run in that order moves together.
  • Only a king (or a run starting with one) can go into an empty column. Face-down cards flip when uncovered.
  • Tap the stock to turn over one card (or three in Draw 3). When it runs out, tap again to turn the waste back over.
  • Tap a card to send it to the best spot, or drag it. When every card is face up, press Finish. Undo is unlimited.
  • Scoring: 10 for each card to a foundation, 5 for a waste card to a column or a flipped card, minus 15 for taking one back off a foundation.
  • Keyboard: D draws, U undoes, H hints.
