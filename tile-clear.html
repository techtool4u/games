How to play
  • Tap a group of two or more touching tiles of the same color to clear it. Tiles above fall down, and empty columns close up to the left.
  • A group of n tiles scores (n − 2)². Big groups are worth far more: 5 tiles score 9, 10 tiles score 64. So build big groups before you clear them.
  • Clear the whole board for a 1,000 point bonus. The game ends when no group of two is left; each leftover tile costs a little.
  • On a computer, point at a group to see what it's worth. Undo takes back a move.
