Bunco

How to play
  • Four players sit at the table, partners facing each other: your team against the other two. There are six rounds, and each round has a target number: 1 in round 1, 2 in round 2, and so on.
  • On your turn, roll three dice. Each die showing the round's number scores 1 point. Keep rolling as long as you score; when a roll scores nothing, the dice pass to the left.
  • Three of a kind that isn't the round's number scores 5. Three of the round's number is a Bunco: 21 points, which ends the round on the spot.
  • The first team to reach 21 points wins the round. After six rounds, the team that won more rounds wins the game. A tie goes to the team with more points, then more Buncos.
  • Keyboard: Space or R rolls.
