How to play
  • Tap two matching tiles to remove them. A tile can only be picked if nothing is stacked on it and its left or right side is open.
  • Tiles match when they are exactly the same. The four flower tiles all match each other, and so do the four season tiles.
  • Every deal can be cleared. If you get stuck, Shuffle rearranges the tiles that are left so the game can still be won.
  • Suits: dots (circles), bamboo (sticks) and characters (red 萬 with a number on top). Also four winds (東 east, 南 south, 西 west, 北 north) and three dragons (red 中, green 發, and the blue-framed white dragon). Small marks in the corner help you tell them apart.
  • Keyboard: arrows move between free tiles, Enter picks, H hint, U undo.
