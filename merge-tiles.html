How to play
  • Swipe (or use the arrow keys) to slide every tile as far as it goes.
  • Two tiles with the same number that run into each other merge into one with their sum, and you score that sum.
  • After every move a new 2 (sometimes a 4) appears in an empty square.
  • Reach the 2048 tile to win, then keep going for bigger tiles if you like. The game ends when no move is possible.
  • Undo takes back your last move.
