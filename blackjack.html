Blackjack

Dealer

You

How to play
  • Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Number cards count their value, face cards 10, and aces 1 or 11.
  • Place a bet with the chips, then deal. You and the dealer get two cards; one of the dealer's is face down.
  • Hit to take a card, Stand to stop. Double doubles your bet for exactly one more card. Split a pair into two hands (once).
  • An ace and a ten-value card on your first two cards is a blackjack and pays 3 to 2. Other wins pay 1 to 1; ties push and your bet comes back.
  • The dealer must hit up to 17. Six decks are shuffled together, and the shoe is reshuffled when it runs low.
  • Keyboard: H hit, S stand, D double, P split, Enter deals.
