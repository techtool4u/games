Liar's Dice

Everyone shakes their dice under a cup. Bid on how many of a number are on the whole table, raise, or call your rival a liar.

How to play
  • Everyone starts with five dice and rolls them in secret. You see only your own.
  • Players take turns bidding on the whole table, like “four 5s” (at least four dice show a 5). Each bid must raise the last: more dice, or the same number of dice on a higher face.
  • Instead of raising, call Liar! All dice are shown. If the bid was wrong the bidder loses a die; if it was right, the caller loses one.
  • With ones are wild on, every 1 also counts as the face being bid, and nobody bids on 1s.
  • Whoever lost a die starts the next round. Lose all your dice and you're out; the last player with dice wins.
