How to play
  • All 52 cards are dealt face up into eight columns. Build the four foundations (top right) up by suit, ace to king, to win.
  • On the columns, stack cards downward in alternating colors: a red 6 on a black 7. Any card can go into an empty column.
  • The four free cells (top left) each hold one card while you dig.
  • You can move a whole run at once if there's room to do it one card at a time: up to (free cells + 1), doubled for each empty column.
  • Tap a card to send it to the best spot, or drag it. Undo is unlimited. Deals are numbered the classic way: deal #11982 is the famous one nobody can win; almost every other deal can be.
  • Keyboard: U undoes, H hints.
