Word Jumble

How to play
  • Unscramble the letters to make a word. Tap letters to place them (or type), tap a placed letter to take it back. Any real word that uses all the letters counts.
  • A game is 10 words. Each word has a timer: solve it fast for a bonus. Points: 10 a letter, plus the seconds left.
  • Hint places the next correct letter for you but halves the word's points. Skip moves on with no points.
  • 2 players take turns, 5 words each, passing the device. The higher score wins.
  • Keyboard: type letters, Backspace takes one back, Enter checks, Space shuffles.
