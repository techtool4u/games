    How to play
    • Each friend has exactly one of each thing, and no two friends share one. Use the clues to work out who has what.
    • Each block of the grid compares two categories. Tap a square once for ✗ (can't be), twice for ✓ (yes), three times to clear.
    • With Auto ✗ on, a ✓ crosses out the rest of its row and column in that block.
    • Houses stand in a row, numbered from left to right. "Next door" means one house either side.
    • Every puzzle can be solved by logic alone. Check shows wrong marks; Hint points at a clue you can use next. Tap a clue to cross it off.
