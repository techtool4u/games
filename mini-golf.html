Mini Golf

How to play
  • Touch the ball (or anywhere), pull back away from where you want it to go, and let go. The further you pull, the harder the shot. The dotted line shows the aim.
  • Get the ball in the cup in as few strokes as you can. Par is the target. A ball that's moving too fast skims over the cup.
  • Sand slows the ball right down. Water costs a stroke and puts you back where you shot from. Bumpers bounce the ball hard.
  • With friends, everyone plays the hole in turn (the furthest from the cup goes next). After 8 strokes you pick up and score 9 on that hole.
  • Lowest total after nine holes wins.
