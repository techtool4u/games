How to play
  • Push every crate onto a red target. Crates turn green when they're in place.
  • You can push one crate at a time by walking into it, but you can't pull, and you can't push two crates in a row. A crate pushed into a corner is stuck, so think ahead.
  • Move with the arrow keys or WASD, swipe on the warehouse, or use the pad. Tap any floor square to walk there, or tap a crate next to you to push it.
  • Undo as much as you like (U or Backspace), or restart (R). "Fewest pushes" is the shortest solution, found by a solver.
  • The levels get harder as you go, from two crates to four.
