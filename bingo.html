Bingo

–
How to play
  • Numbers 1 to 75 are called at random: B is 1 to 15, I 16 to 30, N 31 to 45, G 46 to 60, O 61 to 75.
  • When a number on your card is called, tap it to daub it. The middle square is free.
  • Complete the pattern (any line across, down or diagonally; the four corners; both diagonals; or the whole card), then press BINGO! quickly, before a rival does. A wrong call sits you out for the next three numbers.
  • Online room: 2 to 8 people each play their own card on their own phone. One player hosts and shares a game code; the host's phone calls the numbers and can add computer rivals. The first good BINGO! wins for everyone.
  • Be the caller turns the screen into a caller for a room playing on paper cards: it shows each number big and keeps the board of everything called, so you can check a winner's card.
