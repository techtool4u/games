Viking Chess

How to play
  • The attackers (dark) move first. All pieces, the king too, move like a rook in chess: any distance in a straight line, without jumping.
  • Defenders win if the king reaches one of the four corner squares. Attackers win by surrounding the king on all four sides (three sides are enough if the fourth is the empty throne in the middle).
  • Capture an ordinary piece by trapping it between two of yours, in a line across or down. The corners and the empty throne count as one of your pieces for this. Moving between two enemies is safe.
  • Only the king may stop on the throne or a corner. Other pieces may pass over the empty throne.
  • If a side can't move, it loses. After 150 moves each, the game is a draw.
