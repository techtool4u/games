How to play
  • Clear every square that isn't a mine. A number tells you how many of the eight squares around it hide mines.
  • Your first dig is always safe and opens up an area.
  • Mark mines with flags: switch to Flag mode, long-press a square, or right-click it.
  • Tap a number whose mines are all flagged to dig all its other neighbors at once.
  • With No-guess boards on, every board can be solved by logic alone.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, Space or Enter digs, F flags.
