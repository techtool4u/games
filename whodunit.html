Case log

How to play
  • Someone was done in at the manor. One suspect, one item and one room are sealed in the case file. Every other card is dealt out to the detectives, you included. Be the first to name all three.
  • On your turn, roll and move to a room up to half the roll away (rounded up). The corner rooms have secret passages to the opposite corner. Then suggest a suspect and an item for that room.
  • Going round the table, the first detective who holds one of the three cards must show you one of them, in secret. If nobody can, those cards might be in the case file (or in your own hand).
  • When you're sure, accuse. You can accuse at any point in your turn. Right, and you solve the case. Wrong, and you're out (you still show cards when asked).
  • The notebook fills in what you've been shown, what nobody could show, and what follows from that. Tap a square to add your own ? marks.
