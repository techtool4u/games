Dominoes

Match a number at either end of the line. Empty your hand first to score the pips left in everyone else's.

Scores (first to 100)

How to play
  • A double-six set has 28 tiles. Each player gets 7 tiles (5 each with 3 or 4 players in the Draw game).
  • The first round starts with the highest double. After that, whoever won the last round leads with any tile.
  • On your turn, play a tile whose number matches one of the two open ends. Doubles are placed crosswise. Tap a glowing tile; if it fits both ends, tap the end you want.
  • Draw game: if you can't play, draw from the boneyard until you can (or it runs out, then pass). Block game: if you can't play, you pass.
  • The round ends when someone plays their last tile, scoring every pip left in the other hands. If nobody can play, the lowest hand wins the round and scores the others' pips.
  • Keyboard: arrows pick a tile, Enter plays it, L or R chooses the end, D draws, P passes.
