Sweet Swap

Swap neighboring candies to line up three or more. Bigger matches make special candies that clear whole rows, blast holes or wipe out a color.

How to play
  • Swipe a candy toward a neighbor, or tap two neighbors, to swap them. A swap only counts if it makes a line of 3 or more.
  • Match 4 in a line: a striped candy that clears its whole row or column.
  • Match in an L or T shape: a wrapped candy that blasts the 3×3 square around it.
  • Match 5 in a line: a color bomb. Swap it with any candy to clear every candy of that color.
  • Swap two specials together for a bigger combo. Two color bombs clear the whole board.
  • Pink squares are jelly. Make a match on top to clear it; dark jelly needs two hits.
  • Reach the goal before you run out of moves. Leftover moves turn into bonus points and more stars.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move the yellow box, Space picks a candy, then an arrow swaps it. H shows a hint.
