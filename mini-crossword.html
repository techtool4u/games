How to play
  • Fill every white square so the words answer the clues, across and down.
  • Tap a square to select it. Tap it again (or press Space) to switch between across and down. Tap a clue to jump to it.
  • Type letters with the keyboard or the on-screen keys. Backspace deletes, the arrow keys move, Tab goes to the next clue.
  • Check marks wrong letters; Reveal fills in the answer. Revealed letters are shown in blue.
