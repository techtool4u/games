Word Guess

How to play
  • Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries. Each guess must be a real word.
  • After each guess the tiles change color: green means the letter is in the word and in that spot, gold means it's in the word but somewhere else, and gray means it's not in the word (or not as many times as you used it).
  • Today's word is the same for everyone on the same day. Practice gives a new word whenever you like.
  • Hard mode: any green letter must stay in its spot and any gold letter must be used in later guesses.
  • Type with the keyboard, or tap the keys. Enter submits, Backspace deletes.
