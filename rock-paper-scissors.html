How to play
  • Rock blunts scissors, scissors cut paper, paper wraps rock. The same pick is a tie and doesn't count.
  • Win the majority of rounds to win the match.
  • The learning computer watches what you tend to throw, especially right after you win or lose, and plays to beat it. The more predictable you are, the more it wins. Try to be random!
  • 2 players: each picks in secret. Player 1 picks, hands over the device, then Player 2 picks, and both hands are shown. On a keyboard, Player 1 uses A S D and Player 2 J K L at the same time.
  • Keyboard vs computer: R, P, S (or 1 2 3).
