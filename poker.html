    How to play Texas Hold'em
    • Everyone gets 2 private cards. Five shared cards come in stages: the flop (3), the turn (1) and the river (1).
    • Make the best 5-card hand from your 2 cards plus the 5 shared ones.
    • Before each stage there is a round of betting. Check if nobody has bet, call to match a bet, raise to bet more, or fold to give up the hand.
    • The two players left of the dealer button post the small and big blinds. Blinds go up every 10 hands.
    • Win the pot by having the best hand at the showdown, or by being the last player who hasn't folded.
    • Knock everyone out to win the table. Chips only, no real money.
    • Keyboard: F folds, C checks or calls, R raises, A goes all-in, N deals the next hand.

    Hands, best first
