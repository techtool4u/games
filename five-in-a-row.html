Five in a Row

How to play
  • Black and white take turns placing a stone on any empty point. Black goes first.
  • The first to get five stones in an unbroken line, across, down or diagonally, wins. Six or more in a row also counts.
  • Watch for "open fours" and "double threes": once your rival has two threats at once, you can only block one.
  • The computer gets tougher with each level. Undo takes back your last move (and the computer's reply).
