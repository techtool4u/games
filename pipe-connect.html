How to play
  • Drag from a dot to draw a pipe to the other dot of the same color. Pipes can turn corners but can't cross.
  • Join every pair and fill every square of the grid to win.
  • Drawing over another pipe cuts it back. Drag from the end of a pipe to carry on, or tap a dot to clear its pipe.
  • Hint draws one pipe for you (its route in a known solution).
