How to play
  • Lead the red marker from the top-left corner to the green exit in the bottom right.
  • Swipe, use the arrows or the arrow keys. A swipe runs along a corridor until the next turning.
  • Against the clock, each round's maze is a little bigger. Unused seconds add to your score, and the run ends when time runs out.
  • In the dark, you only see the passages close to you.
