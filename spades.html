Spades

Scores

How to play
  • You and North are partners against West and East. Everyone gets 13 cards.
  • Bidding: each player says how many tricks they expect to win. Your bid and your partner's are added together as your team's contract. Bidding nil means you promise to win no tricks at all.
  • Play: follow the suit led if you can. If you can't, you may play a spade (trump) or anything else. The highest spade wins the trick; with no spades, the highest card of the suit led wins.
  • Spades can't be led until one has been played on another suit, unless you hold only spades.
  • Scoring: make your contract for 10 points per trick bid, plus 1 point (a "bag") for each extra trick. Fall short and you lose 10 per trick bid. Every 10 bags costs 100 points.
  • Nil: +100 if the nil bidder takes no tricks, −100 if they take any. Their tricks don't count toward the partner's bid.
  • First team to the target wins; a team that drops to −200 loses.
  • Keyboard: ← → choose a card, Enter plays it.
