    How to play
    • Computer guesses yours: think of an animal, a food, a plant or an everyday object. Answer each question honestly. If you're not sure, say Not sure. The computer gets twenty questions (its guesses count too).
    • If you stump it, tell it what you were thinking of and it will remember it next time, on this device.
    • You guess the computer's: the computer secretly picks something. Choose questions from the list, then type your guess whenever you're ready. Twenty tries in all.
    • Party tracker: one person thinks of anything at all and the others ask questions out loud. Tap the answer each time to count down the twenty.
    • Keyboard: Y, N, M (maybe) and U (not sure) answer the computer's questions.
