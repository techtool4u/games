Fox and Geese

How to play
  • Thirteen geese try to trap one fox on a cross-shaped board. The geese move first.
  • A goose moves one step along a line: down or sideways, never back up. Geese can't jump.
  • The fox moves one step in any direction along a line, or jumps over a goose to an empty point just beyond it, capturing the goose. It can keep jumping in the same turn.
  • The geese win if the fox can't move. The fox wins by capturing 6 geese (then too few are left to trap it), or if the geese can't move.
  • Tap a piece, then a green dot. During a chain of jumps, tap the fox or "End the fox's turn" to stop.
