    How to play
    • Each side has six pits and a store. Your pits are the bottom row and your store is on the right.
    • On your turn, pick one of your pits. Its seeds are sown one at a time, counterclockwise, into the following pits and your store. Your rival's store is skipped.
    • Last seed in your store: take another turn.
    • Last seed in an empty pit on your side, with seeds in the pit straight across: capture both into your store.
    • When either row runs out of seeds, the game ends and each player banks the seeds left on their own side. Most seeds in the store wins.
    • Shortcuts: keys 1–6 play your pits from left to right, U undoes.
