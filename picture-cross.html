How to play
  • Fill squares to reveal a hidden picture. The numbers on each row and column give the runs of filled squares in that line, in order. "3 1" means a run of 3, then at least one gap, then a run of 1.
  • Tap a square to fill it. Switch to Cross (or right-click, or press X) to mark squares you know are empty. Drag to fill or cross a whole run at once. Tap again to clear.
  • A clue turns gray when its line matches. Every puzzle can be solved by logic alone, with no guessing.
  • Show mistakes outlines any filled square that shouldn't be.
