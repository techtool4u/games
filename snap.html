Snap!

How to play
  • All the cards are dealt out face down. Players take turns flipping their top card onto the middle pile.
  • When the top two cards on the pile have the same rank (two 7s, two kings…), be the first to hit SNAP! and you win the whole pile.
  • Snap at the wrong moment and you pay a card to the pile.
  • Run out of cards and you're out. Win every card to win the game, or have the most when the 3-minute timer ends.
  • Keyboard: Space or S snaps, P pauses.
