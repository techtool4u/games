How to play
  • Tapping a light switches it and the lights directly above, below, left and right of it. On becomes off and off becomes on.
  • Switch every light off. "Fewest possible" is the smallest number of taps that solves this puzzle, worked out exactly.
  • Tapping the same light twice undoes it, and the order of taps never matters.
  • Hint outlines a light that is part of a shortest solution from where you are now.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move, Space or Enter taps.
