Ship, Captain & Crew

Your turn
How to play
  • You get three rolls of five dice per turn. You need a 6 (the ship), then a 5 (the captain), then a 4 (the crew), in that order. They're set aside for you as soon as you roll them in order: a 5 doesn't count until you have your 6.
  • Once you have all three, the other two dice are your cargo, and their total is your score for the round. If you have rolls left, you can keep the cargo or roll both cargo dice again (and take whatever comes).
  • No ship, captain and crew after three rolls means no cargo: 0 for the round.
  • Each round, everyone takes one turn. After the last round, the most cargo wins. The player with the round's best cargo is marked with a star.
  • Keyboard: R or Space rolls, K or Enter keeps the cargo.
