    How to play
    • Red moves first. Pieces stand on the points where lines cross. Checkmate the enemy general to win.
    • Having no legal move at all also loses, even if you are not in check.
    • The two generals may never face each other on the same file with nothing between them.
    • Tap a piece to see where it can go, then tap a marked point.
    • Repeating the same position three times is a draw, unless one side was checking on every move of the repeat: that side loses. Sixty moves each with no capture is also a draw.
    • Moves are written in the common WXF style: piece, file (counted 1 to 9 from each player's right), then + forward, - back or = sideways, and the steps or the file it lands on.
    • Keyboard: arrows move the blue box, Enter picks and places, U undoes, F flips.
