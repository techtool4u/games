Pyramid Solitaire

How to play
  • Remove pairs of cards that add up to 13. Aces count 1, jacks 11, queens 12; a king is 13 on its own, so tap it once to remove it.
  • A pyramid card can be used only when no cards are covering it from the row below.
  • Tap the stock to turn up a new card onto the waste. The top waste card can pair with any uncovered pyramid card.
  • Clear the whole pyramid to win. When the stock runs out you may turn the waste over again if you still have passes left.
  • Scoring: 5 for each pyramid card removed, 25 for each row cleared, and a bonus for clearing the pyramid early.
  • Keyboard: D draws, U undoes, H hints.
