How to play
  • Run right through three stages (dusk jungle, the factory, the city rooftops), blast everything in your way, and beat the guardian machine at the end of each.
  • Keyboard: ← → or A D to run, ↑ or W to aim up (with ← → for diagonals), ↓ or S to aim down while jumping, Z / Space / J to jump, X / K to fire (hold it to keep firing).
  • Touch: the left buttons run and aim up; the right buttons jump and fire.
  • Walkers patrol, turrets shoot at you, and drones swoop in. Spikes and pits are trouble.
  • Pick up S for a spread shot, R for rapid fire, and + for a heart. Losing a life takes your power-up away.
  • Flags are checkpoints. P pauses.
