How to play
  • Your pizzeria makes pizzas on a line: dough → sauce → cheese → oven → boxes. Every finished box is sold.
  • The line only goes as fast as its slowest station (marked “slowest”). Upgrade that one to sell more pizzas a second.
  • Every 25 levels a station doubles its speed.
  • Tap Bake a pizza to make one by hand. Quick hands makes each tap count for more.
  • New menu pizzas sell for more. Customers always order the best pizza you know.
  • When a rush order pops up over the line, tap it: everything sells for double for 30 seconds.
  • Once you've earned a million coins you can open a new shop: start over with gold stars, each one making every pizza worth 10% more.
  • The kitchen keeps working (at half speed) while you're away, for up to 4 hours.
