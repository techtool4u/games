Air Hockey

How to play
  • Slide your mallet to hit the puck into the goal at the far end. Your mallet stays in your half.
  • Hit the puck hard by moving the mallet fast; it bounces off the side rails. A goal goes to whoever scored it, and the other player serves.
  • You're the red mallet at the bottom: drag anywhere in the bottom half, or move the mouse.
  • Two players: each drags in their own half of the table. Fingers work at the same time.
