How to play
  • Each turn: draw one card (from the stock, or the top of the discard pile), then make any melds you can, then discard one card.
  • A meld is a set of three or four of a kind, or a run of three or more in a row in one suit (ace is low or high: A-2-3 or Q-K-A).
  • Select cards in your hand and press Meld. To add a card to any meld on the table, select it and tap that meld (or press Lay off).
  • You can't throw back the discard you just picked up.
  • Get rid of all your cards to win the hand. You score the cards left in everyone else's hand: face cards 10, aces 1, others their number.
