How to play
  • Each player gets six cards and puts two face down into the crib, which belongs to the dealer. The deal alternates each hand.
  • A starter card is cut. If it's a jack, the dealer pegs 2 (his heels).
  • Pegging: take turns playing a card and calling the running total, which can't pass 31. Score 2 for making 15 or 31, 2 for a pair, 6 for three of a kind, 12 for four, and 1 per card for a run of three or more (in any order). If you can't play without passing 31, you say "go" and your opponent plays on; the last card before a reset scores 1.
  • The show: the non-dealer counts first, then the dealer, then the crib, each with the starter: 2 for each combination adding to 15, 2 per pair, 1 per card in runs, 4 for a flush in the hand (5 with the starter; the crib needs all 5), and 1 for the jack of the starter's suit (nobs).
  • Picture cards count 10 and aces 1. First to 121 (or 61) wins, even in the middle of a hand.
