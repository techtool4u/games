Tap a peg to lift its top disc, then tap where it goes.
How to play
  • Move the whole stack from the left peg to the right peg.
  • Move one disc at a time, and never put a bigger disc on a smaller one.
  • Tap a peg to lift its top disc, then tap another peg to drop it. You can also drag a disc, or use the keys 1, 2 and 3.
  • The fewest moves for n discs is 2ⁿ − 1: 7 for three discs, 255 for eight.
  • Show me finishes the puzzle from wherever you are, the quickest way.
