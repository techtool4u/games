    How to play
    • Tap a piece to see where it can go, then tap a highlighted square. You can also drag pieces.
    • All the standard rules apply: castling, en passant and pawn promotion (you choose the piece).
    • Checkmate wins. Stalemate, threefold repetition, 50 moves without a capture or pawn move, and too little material to mate are draws.
    • Levels: Easy makes beginner mistakes, Medium plays sensibly, Hard calculates a few moves deep, Expert thinks longer and knows some openings.
    • Undo takes back your last move (and the computer's reply). Hint shows a good move for you.
    • An unfinished game is saved, so you can come back to it.
    • Online: play a friend on another phone or computer, connected directly with no game server. One of you hosts and the other joins by scanning a QR code or pasting a code. Colors swap each game; Undo and Hint are off, and you can resign. If the connection drops, connect again and the game carries on.
    • Keyboard: arrows move the blue box, Enter picks a piece and its destination, U undoes, F flips.
