Billiards 3D

This device can't show 3D graphics. Play the 2D table instead.

Drag right, let go to shoot
How to play
  • 8-ball pool. Break the rack, then one player takes the solids (1–7) and the other the stripes (9–15), decided by the first ball potted after the break.
  • Aim by dragging sideways on the table: the camera turns with your cue. The dotted line and ghost ball show where the cue ball will hit. ◀ ▶ fine-tune the aim. Top view looks straight down; there, the cue points where your finger is.
  • Shoot by dragging the power bar to the right and letting go. Slide back to the left before letting go to cancel.
  • Pot one of your balls and you shoot again. Clear your group, then pot the 8 to win.
  • Fouls (the cue ball going in, hitting the other group or the 8 first, hitting nothing, or no ball reaching a cushion) give your opponent ball in hand: they can place the cue ball anywhere. After the break it goes behind the line.
  • Pot the 8 before your group is cleared, or on a foul, and you lose.
  • Online: one player hosts, the other joins with the game code. 2D and 3D tables can play each other.
  • When you have ball in hand, drag the cue ball (in Top view it's easiest) to place it.
  • Keyboard: arrows aim (Shift for bigger steps), hold Space to build power and let go to shoot.
