Potion Pour

Level 1
How to play
  • Sort the potions so every flask holds just one color, filled to the top.
  • Tap a flask to lift it, then tap another flask to pour. You can pour onto the same color or into an empty flask, as long as there's room. All the matching layers on top pour together.
  • Undo takes back a pour. Hint shows a good next pour. Flask adds one empty flask, once per level. Restart starts the level again.
  • From level 15, some levels hide the lower layers under cloudy glass ( ? ). A hidden layer shows its color when it reaches the top.
  • Symbols marks each color with its own shape, which helps if some colors look alike.
  • Every level has a solution. Your level and best scores are saved. Pick any level you've reached from Levels.
  • Keyboard: number keys 1–9 and 0 pick flasks (or arrows and Enter), U undoes, H shows a hint.
