Five Dice

Roll five dice up to three times a turn, keep the ones you like, and score them in one box of your card. Thirteen turns, highest total wins.

Your turnRound 1 of 13

How to play
  • Each turn, roll up to three times. Tap dice to hold them between rolls; held dice stay put.
  • Then tap one empty box in your column to score the dice there. Every box is used once, so sometimes you must take a zero.
  • Upper section: add up the dice of that number. Reach 63 or more across the six boxes for a 35-point bonus.
  • Lower section: three or four of a kind (sum of all dice), full house (25), small straight of four (30), large straight of five (40), five of a kind (50) and chance (any dice, sum).
  • Every extra five of a kind after scoring 50 earns a 100-point bonus, and can fill a lower box as a wild card when its number's upper box is used.
  • Keyboard: Space or R rolls, 1–5 hold dice, arrows pick a box, Enter scores.
