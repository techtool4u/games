How to play
  • Black plays first. Place one stone per turn on any empty intersection. Stones never move.
  • Stones of one color that touch along the lines form a group. When a group has no empty points next to it, it is captured and removed.
  • You can't play where your stone would have no liberties, unless it captures. You also can't immediately retake a single stone in a ko: play elsewhere first.
  • When neither side can gain anything, both pass. Then mark any hopeless (dead) stones by tapping them. The computer marks its best guess for you.
  • Score is area: your stones on the board plus the empty points only you surround. White gets 7.5 points of komi for moving second.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, Enter or Space places, P passes, U undoes.
