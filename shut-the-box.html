Shut the Box

Your turn
How to play
  • Roll two dice, then flip down (shut) any open tiles that add up to the total: a 9 could be 9, 8+1, 5+4, 6+2+1 and so on.
  • Keep rolling and shutting until you roll a total you can't make. Your score is the sum of the tiles still open; lower is better.
  • Shut every tile and you've shut the box: an instant win.
  • Once all the tiles from 7 up are shut, you may roll just one die.
  • With more than one player, each takes one turn on a fresh box. The lowest score wins.
  • Keyboard: R or Space rolls (1 rolls one die), number keys pick tiles (0 for 10, − and = for 11 and 12), Enter shuts.
