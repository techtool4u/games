Euchre

Scores

How to play
  • You and North are partners against West and East. Euchre uses only the 9s through aces: 24 cards. Everyone gets five, and one card is turned up.
  • Choosing trump: going around from the dealer's left, each player can order up the turned card's suit as trump (the dealer then picks that card up and discards one) or pass. If all four pass, each may name a different suit, or pass. The dealer can't pass the second time around ("stick the dealer").
  • The team that picks trump are the makers. A player who is very strong can go alone: their partner sits out the hand.
  • The bowers: the jack of trump (the right bower) is the highest card, and the other jack of the same color (the left bower) is second and counts as a trump. Then ace, king, queen, 10, 9 of trump.
  • Follow the suit led if you can (the left bower belongs to the trump suit). The highest trump wins, otherwise the highest card of the suit led.
  • Scoring: makers who win 3 or 4 tricks score 1; all 5 (a march) scores 2, or 4 when going alone. Makers who win fewer than 3 are euchred, and the other team scores 2.
