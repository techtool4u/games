How to play
  • Players take turns dropping a disc into one of the seven columns. It falls to the lowest empty space.
  • Get four of your discs in a line: across, up and down, or diagonally.
  • If the frame fills up with no four in a row, it's a draw.
  • Red always moves first. Tip: the middle column is part of the most possible lines.
  • Online: play a friend on another phone or computer, connected directly with no game server. One of you hosts and the other joins by scanning a QR code or pasting a code. You take turns being red (who goes first). If the connection drops, connect again and the game carries on.
  • Keyboard: ← → choose a column, Enter or Space drops, 1–7 drop into that column, U undoes.
