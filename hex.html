Hex

Take turns claiming cells. Red tries to join the two red sides, blue the two blue sides. Someone always wins: there are no draws.

How to play
  • Red moves first. On your turn, claim any empty cell.
  • Red wins by building an unbroken chain of red cells between the two red sides. Blue wins with a chain between the blue sides. Corner cells touch both.
  • The board can never fill up without someone winning, so there are no draws.
  • Swap rule: going first is a big edge, so after red's first move, blue may press Swap to take that cell for themselves (it is mirrored across the board). Then red moves again. This keeps red from opening in the center.
  • Tip: two of your cells with two empty cells between them form a bridge. If your rival takes one gap, take the other and they stay linked.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, Enter or Space claims, U undoes.
