Coil Arena

How to play
  • Steer your coil around the arena and eat the glowing orbs to grow longer.
  • If your head runs into another coil's body, you're out. Make other coils run into yours: when they're knocked out they burst into a trail of big orbs.
  • Heads-on, the longer coil wins. The arena wall is deadly too.
  • Boost to dash: it's how you cut in front of rivals, but it costs a little length while you hold it.
  • Phone: drag anywhere to steer (your coil heads the way you drag) and hold the Boost button. Computer: the coil follows the mouse; hold the mouse button or Space to boost. Arrow keys or A/D also turn.
  • Your score is the longest you grew. P pauses.
