Gin Rummy

Scores

How to play
  • Each player gets 10 cards. Make melds: three or four of a kind, or runs of three or more in one suit (ace is low). Cards not in a meld are deadwood; face cards count 10, aces 1.
  • On your turn, draw the top card of the stock or the discard pile, then discard one card. You can't throw back a card you just took from the discards.
  • To start, the player who didn't deal may take the first face-up card. If they pass, the dealer may take it; if both pass, the non-dealer draws from the stock.
  • Knock when your deadwood is 10 or less after discarding: hands are shown, and your opponent may lay off deadwood on your melds. You score the difference in deadwood.
  • If the defender's deadwood is equal or lower, that's an undercut: they score the difference plus 25.
  • Gin (no deadwood) scores 25 plus the opponent's deadwood, and nothing can be laid off. Big gin (all 11 cards after drawing) scores 31 plus.
  • If only two cards are left in the stock, the hand is a draw. First to the target wins and gets 100 for the game plus 25 for every hand won.
  • Your hand is arranged for you: melds first (underlined in green), then deadwood. Tap a card to pick it, then Discard or Knock. Keyboard: ← → choose, Enter discards, S / D draw from stock / discards.
