How to play
  • You're a firefly in a hedge maze at night. Collect every glow drop to clear the maze. There are three mazes, and they come round again faster.
  • Beetles hunt you, each with its own habit: red chases you, purple cuts you off ahead, blue flanks you, and green wanders. Every so often they tire and head for their corners. If one catches you, you lose a life.
  • Eat a big silver moon drop and the beetles get dizzy for a few seconds: now you can catch them, for 200, 400, 800 and 1,600 points in a row. A caught beetle scuttles back to its burrow and comes out again.
  • The openings at the sides lead round to the other side of the maze. Beetles slow down in them.
  • Garden treats (berries, acorns, mushrooms, honey…) appear under the burrow twice a maze for bonus points.
  • Steer with the arrow keys or WASD, by swiping, or with the pad; you can press a direction early. An extra life comes at 10,000 points. P pauses.
