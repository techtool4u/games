How to play
  • You're a firefly in a hedge maze at night. Collect every glow drop to light up the garden and move on to the next level, a brand-new maze.
  • Beetles hunt you through the hedges, and each one has its own habit: the red one chases you, the purple one cuts you off ahead, the blue one flanks from the side, and the green one wanders. Every so often they tire and drift off to their corners for a rest. If one catches you, you lose a life.
  • Pick up a lantern to store a light. Press Light! (or Space) to flash it: every beetle near you is stunned for a few seconds and can't hurt you. Flying into a stunned beetle sends it back to its burrow for bonus points.
  • Steer with the arrow keys or WASD, by swiping on the maze, or with the pad. You can press a direction early and the firefly turns at the next opening.
  • Clearing a maze earns an extra life (up to 5). P pauses.
