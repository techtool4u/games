How to play
  • All cards start face down. Flip two per turn.
  • If they match, they stay face up, you score a pair and go again. If not, they flip back and the next player goes.
  • When every pair is found, the most pairs wins. Playing solo, try to finish in as few turns as possible.
  • The computer remembers cards it has seen, sometimes. On Perfect memory it never forgets.
  • Online: play a friend on another phone or computer. One of you hosts and the other joins by scanning a QR code (or pasting a code). The devices connect directly to each other, with no game server; the same Wi-Fi works best. The host picks the board and pictures, and you take turns starting.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, Enter or Space flips.
