How to play
  • Jump one peg over a neighbor into an empty hole straight beyond it; the peg you jumped over is removed.
  • Jumps go along the lines of holes (across or down; on the triangle, along its three directions).
  • Tap a peg, then a highlighted hole. Keep jumping until no jump is left.
  • The goal is to finish with a single peg, best of all in the hole that started empty. Fewer pegs left is better.
  • Hint looks for a jump from which the board can still be cleared down to one peg.
