Darts

How to play
  • Throwing: hold your finger (or the mouse) on the board. The crosshair sits a little above your finger so you can see it, and it sways like a real hand. Let go to throw. Holding too long makes the sway grow. Keyboard: arrows aim, Space throws.
  • Each turn is three darts. The outer ring scores double, the inner ring treble; the green ring is 25 and the bullseye 50.
  • 501 / 301: count down to exactly zero. With Double out, the last dart must land in a double (or the bullseye). Going below zero (or to 1 with double out) is a bust: the turn doesn't count. From 170 down, the status line suggests a checkout.
  • Cricket: only 15 to 20 and the bull count. Hit a number three times (a double counts 2, a treble 3) to close it. Once closed, more hits on it score points while any rival still has it open. Close everything with the most points to win.
  • Around the Clock: hit 1, then 2, and so on to 20, then the bull. Any part of the number counts.
  • Play the computer, pass one device between 2 to 4 players, or play a friend online with a game code.
