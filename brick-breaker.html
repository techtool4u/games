How to play
  • Move the paddle to bounce the ball into the bricks. Drag anywhere on the screen, move the mouse, or use the arrow keys. Tap or press Space to launch.
  • Where the ball hits the paddle sets its angle: near the edges sends it out wide.
  • Silver bricks take two hits, gold ones three. Clear every brick to reach the next level (there are 8).
  • Some bricks drop a power-up: W widens the paddle, M splits the ball into three, S slows it down, + is an extra ball.
  • You start with 3 balls. P or Escape pauses.
