Corner Market

0
How to play
  • You run a little corner market. Walk by dragging anywhere on the shop (your shopkeeper walks the way you drag), or with the arrow keys / WASD.
  • Walk over ripe produce in the garden to pick it up (you can carry a few at a time), then walk to its shelf to stock it.
  • Customers take what they want from the shelves and queue at the till. Stand on the green till mat to take their money. If a shelf stays empty too long, they leave without buying.
  • Stand on a dashed unlock pad to pay for it bit by bit: new produce, a bigger basket, faster shoes, a cashier, and a farmhand who restocks for you.
  • The shop saves itself, so you can come back any time.
