How to play
  • Turn the top word into the bottom word by changing one letter at a time. Every step must be a real English word.
  • Type your next word (on the keyboard below or your own), then press Enter. The changed letter is highlighted.
  • Par is the fewest steps possible. Hints reveal the next word on a shortest route from where you are.
  • Undo takes back your last step.
