Bridges

How to play
  • Connect the islands with bridges so that each island has exactly as many bridges as its number.
  • Bridges run straight across or down, never cross each other, and two islands can share at most two bridges.
  • All the islands must end up joined into one group.
  • Drag from an island to a neighbor to build a bridge (drag again for a second one, a third time to remove them), or tap one island and then the other.
  • Islands turn green when they have the right number of bridges and red when they have too many. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.
