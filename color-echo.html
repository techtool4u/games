Color Echo

How to play
  • Watch the pads light up and listen to the notes, then tap the same pads in the same order.
  • Each round adds one more to the end of the sequence, and it plays a little faster as you go.
  • Classic: one slip and the sequence plays again for one more try. Strict: one slip ends the game. Reverse: play the sequence back to front.
  • Keyboard: 1 2 3 4, or Q W A S, for green, red, yellow and blue.
