How to play
  • Get rid of all your cards first. With two players everyone gets seven cards, otherwise five.
  • On your turn, play a card that matches the top of the discard pile by suit or rank.
  • Eights are wild: play one any time and choose the suit the next player must follow.
  • Can't play? Tap the draw pile. If the card you draw fits, you may play it; otherwise your turn ends.
  • The winner of a round scores the cards left in everyone else's hand: 50 for an eight, 10 for a face card, 1 for an ace, and face value for the rest.
  • Keyboard: ← → choose a card, Enter plays it, D draws.
