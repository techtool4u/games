    How to play
    • Tap a tile in your rack, then tap a square to place it. Tap a placed tile to take it back. You can also drag tiles.
    • Each turn, place tiles in one straight line to make a word that joins the words already on the board. The first word must cover the ★.
    • Every new word you form must be a real word. Score is the letter values plus bonus squares; using all 7 tiles adds 50.
    • Blank tiles can be any letter and score 0. Swap tiles or pass if you're stuck.
    • The game ends when the bag is empty and someone uses their last tile, or after six scoreless turns in a row. Leftover tiles count against you.
    • The computer's Easy and Normal players only use common words; Hard knows the full dictionary.
