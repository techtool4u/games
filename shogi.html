    How to play
    • Sente (☗, the first player) starts at the bottom. Pieces point toward the opponent. Checkmate the opposing king to win.
    • Tap a piece to see where it can go, then tap a highlighted square. To drop a captured piece, tap it in your tray and then an empty square.
    • Promotion: a piece moving into, out of, or within the far three rows may promote (it flips over and its name turns red). Pawns, lances and knights must promote if they could never move again.
    • Drops: you can't drop a pawn on a column that already has one of your unpromoted pawns, or drop a pawn to give immediate checkmate. Pawns, lances and knights can't be dropped where they could never move.
    • The same position four times is a draw (sennichite), unless one side was giving check every move, in which case that side loses.
    • Keyboard: arrows move the blue box, Enter picks and places, U undoes, F flips.
