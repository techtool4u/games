    How to play
    • Pieces move one square diagonally forward onto an empty dark square.
    • Jump over an enemy piece to an empty square right behind it to capture it. Keep jumping with the same piece while you can.
    • With “Jumps are forced” on, you must capture when you can. That's the official rule and the default.
    • Reach the far row to crown a king. Kings move and jump backward too. Crowning ends the turn.
    • You win when your rival has no pieces or no legal move. 40 moves each with only kings moving and no capture is a draw.
    • Squares are numbered 1–32 from Black's side, so moves read like “11-15” or “15x22”.
